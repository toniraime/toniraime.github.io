Every night at The Container can be lived five different ways. The room, the lineup and the Funktion-One sound are the same for everyone — what changes is where you stand, how close you are to the DJ, and how much comfort and service surround you.

From the open energy of the main floor to a fully private VIP Box with its own waiter, this page explains each ticket in plain words — so you know exactly what you're booking before the night.

Ticket packages

The five
experiences

Get tickets
Ticket package · 01

General Admission

Experience The Container from the heart of the main floor.

GA is the main floor — straight in front of the DJ, closest to the main sound system, right in the middle of the energy. It comes with access to the outdoor F&B area and dedicated GA restrooms.

  • Direct front view of the DJ
  • Closest to the main sound system
  • Access to the outdoor F&B area
  • Dedicated GA restrooms
Ticket package · 02

Backstage

Get closer to the performance.

The closest position to the DJ in the venue. Backstage also unlocks the second-floor VIP mezzanine, with its dedicated VIP F&B area, VIP restrooms on the second floor and outdoors, and VIP parking.

  • Close proximity to the DJ
  • Access to the second-floor VIP mezzanine
  • Dedicated VIP F&B area
  • VIP restrooms on the second floor & outdoors
  • VIP parking
VIP Box access is not included.
Ticket package · 03

VIP Standing

An elevated view with added comfort.

A place on the second-floor mezzanine, looking over the DJ and the main floor from above — the full show, with breathing room and the complete set of VIP amenities.

  • Second-floor mezzanine access
  • Elevated view of the DJ & main floor
  • Dedicated VIP F&B area
  • VIP restrooms on the second floor & outdoors
  • VIP parking upon availability
VIP Box access is not included.
Ticket package · 04

VIP Tables

Your reserved space on the second-floor mezzanine.

Your group's own reserved high table on the mezzanine — a fixed home base for the night, with an elevated view of the DJ and the main floor and the full set of VIP amenities around it.

  • Reserved high table for your group
  • Elevated view of the DJ & main floor
  • Dedicated VIP F&B area
  • VIP restrooms on the second floor & outdoors
  • VIP parking
VIP Box access is not included.
Ticket package · 05

VIP Boxes

Our most private and premium experience.

A private space with its own table and dedicated waiter service, complimentary drinks, access to every VIP area and service in the venue, fast-track entry at the door and complimentary valet parking.

  • Private space & table
  • Dedicated waiter service
  • Complimentary drinks
  • Access to all VIP areas & services
  • Fast-track venue entry
  • Complimentary valet parking
VIP Box access is exclusive to guests who book a VIP Box.
