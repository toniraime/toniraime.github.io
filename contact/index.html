FAQ

What is The Container?

A purpose-built entertainment venue with a double life. By day: art galleries, fashion exhibitions, the cinema club, live performances and workshops. By night: a music club on a Funktion-One sound system. Same box, two worlds.

Where is The Container?

North Obhur, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. The exact pin and directions are shared with every event announcement.

When do events start and finish?

Day programming usually runs from late morning into the evening. Musical nights open late and run until… late. Exact times are listed on each event.

How do I get tickets?

Ticket sales move to our official booking partner very soon. Until then, join the list on the events page and follow @thecontainerlive — you'll be first to know when booking opens.

Is there an age limit?

Musical nights are 18+ with valid ID. Day programming is open to everyone unless an event says otherwise.

Can I book The Container for a private or brand event?

Yes — the box is modular by nature. Send us a message through the form with your idea, dates and audience size, and our team will come back to you.

Press, artists & collaborations?

We love ambitious ideas. Use the form and tell us what you're thinking — exhibitions, showcases, residencies, brand takeovers.

Contact us

We usually reply within 48 hours. Prefer email? Write to support@thecontainer.group — or DM @thecontainerlive.
