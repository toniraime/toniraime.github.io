The story

One box.
A double life.

Built for culture
and entertainment

A flexible venue for day and night programming. The Container is a purpose-built cultural venue in North Obhur, Jeddah. Constructed across three floors using shipping containers, steel and concrete, the space is designed to accommodate different formats, audiences and experiences.

By day, The Container hosts workshops, exhibitions, talks, seminars and creative gatherings. It provides a flexible setting for learning, collaboration and new ideas.

At night, the programming includes music, fashion shows, stand-up comedy, art exhibitions and live performances, supported by Funktion-One sound and immersive production.

The venue can also accommodate private events, brand activations and special productions, bringing culture, entertainment and community together in one adaptable space.
